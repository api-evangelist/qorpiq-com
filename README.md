QorpIQ provides a comprehensive platform for Indian company and director data, offering searchable MCA filings, KYB verification, GST verification, and watchlist monitoring. Users can access APIs to retrieve company details, director information, incorporation dates, and financial documents, supporting compliance, risk assessment, and business intelligence workflows across India.

**Areas:** Company

## QorpIQ on APIs.io

Everything profiled for QorpIQ is published on APIs.io rather than in this repository. Start at the provider page — **[apis.io/providers/qorpiq-com/](https://apis.io/providers/qorpiq-com/)** — or go straight to one of the APIs or properties below.

### APIs (8)

- [QorpIQ Account API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-account-api/) — Check your balance. Free to call.
- [QorpIQ Bulk API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-bulk-api/) — Run one check over up to 25 items in a single call.
- [QorpIQ Directors API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-directors-api/) — Check a director by DIN or PAN: status and disqualification, their MCA record, and every company they direct.
- [QorpIQ Due Diligence API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-due-diligence-api/) — Answer the questions a lender or onboarding team asks about a company: charges, filing health, turnover, MCA penalties and who can sign.
- [QorpIQ Filings API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-filings-api/) — Structured data from the company's filed forms: financial statements, annual return, allotments, director changes and the filings list.
- [QorpIQ Lookup API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-lookup-api/) — Find a company by name, read its MCA record and directors, and get its PAN.
- [QorpIQ More API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-more-api/) — Endpoints kept for existing integrations. Not shown in the developer docs.
- [QorpIQ Tax IDs API](https://apis.io/apis/qorpiq-com/qorpiq-com-tax-ids-api/) — Verify a PAN, get the business behind a company PAN, check GST registration and returns, and look up the LEI.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for QorpIQ: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
